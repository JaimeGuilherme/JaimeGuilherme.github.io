Thesis project

Georeferencing satellite images with a U-Net

Undergraduate thesis at IME, defended October 2025, built with two classmates

A deep learning pipeline that aligns CBERS-4A satellite images to the map automatically. A U-Net finds road intersections in the image, and those points are matched to the same intersections in OpenStreetMap.

  1. CBERS-4A image, pan-sharpened to 2 m
  2. U-Net segments road intersections
  3. Detected points are extracted from the masks
  4. Points are matched to OpenStreetMap intersections
  5. Ground control points go to QGIS for georeferencing
  • Trained in PyTorch with focal loss, and evaluated with IoU, precision, recall and F1.
  • Training masks were generated from OpenStreetMap road data, cleaned with a custom QGIS processing script.
  • Matching uses nearest neighbor and Hungarian assignment inside a 20 m radius, and the output is a points file the QGIS Georeferencer reads directly.
